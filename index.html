KNOW THE REQUIREMENTS

The Financial Benchmark: The primary applicant needs a minimum pension of just $1,000 per month

A Reliable Lifetime Income: You must prove you receive a lifetime guaranteed pension from a government entity (like US Social Security), a private company, or a social security system. Note: Guaranteed temporary pensions do not qualify for this specific visa.

Bringing Family? If you are including your spouse or dependents, you simply need to show an additional $250 per month in income for each dependent included in the application

Proof of Income: You'll need an apostilled pension certificate from your provider , alongside proof of those income deposits via bank statements or official letters.

Standard Identification & Security: A valid passport and an apostilled criminal background check from your current country of residence.
